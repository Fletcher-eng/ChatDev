# Biology key terms

Every key term in the Specpoint Biology notes. Terms marked **S** are Supplement (Extended) content.

### A
- [[Active transport]]: The movement of particles through a cell membrane from a region of lower concentration to a region of higher concentration, against a concentration gradient, using energy from respiration.
- [[Animal cell]]: Has a nucleus, cytoplasm, cell membrane, mitochondria and ribosomes.

### B
- [[Bacterial cell]]: A single celled organism with no nucleus.

### C
- [[Cell membrane]]: A partially permeable layer around every cell.
- [[Cell wall]]: A tough outer layer that strengthens the cell and keeps its shape.
- [[Cellulose]]: A carbohydrate (a polysaccharide made of glucose) that forms plant cell walls.
- [[Chloroplasts]]: Contain chlorophyll, which absorbs light energy for photosynthesis.
- [[Ciliated cell]]: Lines the trachea and bronchi.
- [[Circular DNA]]: Bacteria have a single circular chromosome loose in the cytoplasm instead of a nucleus.
- [[Concentration gradient]]: The difference in concentration between two regions.
- [[Cytoplasm]]: The fluid inside the cell membrane where most of the cell's chemical reactions take place.

### D
- [[Diffusion]]: The net movement of particles from a region of higher concentration to a region of lower concentration, down a concentration gradient, as a result of their random movement.

### F
- [[Flaccid]] **S**: Describes a plant cell that has lost water and no longer presses on its cell wall.

### K
- [[Kinetic energy]]: The energy of movement.

### L
- [[Large permanent vacuole]]: A large space filled with cell sap.

### M
- [[Magnification]]: How many times bigger an image is than the real object.
- [[Micrometre]] **S**: A unit of length used for cells.
- [[Mitochondria]]: The site of aerobic respiration, which releases energy for the cell.

### N
- [[Neurone]]: Conducts electrical impulses.
- [[Nucleus]]: Contains the genetic material, as chromosomes, which controls the activities of the cell.

### O
- [[Organ]]: A structure made of a group of tissues, working together to perform specific functions.
- [[Organ system]]: A group of organs with related functions, working together to perform body functions.
- [[Osmosis]]: The diffusion of water through a partially permeable membrane.

### P
- [[Palisade mesophyll cell]]: Packed with chloroplasts to absorb as much light as possible for photosynthesis.
- [[Partially permeable membrane]]: A membrane that lets some molecules through, such as water, but not others, such as large solute molecules.
- [[Percentage change]]: Percentage change = (change in mass ÷ starting mass) × 100.
- [[Photosynthesis]]: The process by which plants make carbohydrates from raw materials using energy from light.
- [[Plant cell]]: Has everything an animal cell has, plus a cell wall made of cellulose, chloroplasts and a large permanent vacuole.
- [[Plasmids]]: Small extra rings of DNA found in bacteria.
- [[Plasmolysis]] **S**: When a plant cell loses so much water by osmosis that the cell membrane pulls away from the cell wall.
- [[Protein carriers]] **S**: Proteins in the cell membrane that move molecules or ions across it during active transport.

### R
- [[Red blood cell]]: Transports oxygen using haemoglobin.
- [[Respiration]]: The chemical reactions in cells that break down nutrient molecules and release energy.
- [[Ribosomes]]: The site of protein synthesis.
- [[Root hair cell]]: Its long extension gives a large surface area for absorbing water by osmosis and mineral ions by active transport.

### S
- [[Specialised cell]]: A cell whose structure is adapted to carry out one particular function.
- [[Sperm and egg cells]]: The gametes, used for reproduction.
- [[Surface area]]: A larger surface area lets more particles cross at once, so it increases the rate of diffusion.

### T
- [[Tissue]]: A group of cells with similar structures, working together to perform a shared function.
- [[Turgid]] **S**: Describes a plant cell that is full of water, with its contents pressing outwards on the cell wall.
- [[Turgor pressure]] **S**: The pressure of water inside a plant cell pushing outwards on the cell wall.

### W
- [[Water potential]] **S**: Describes how freely water molecules can move.
