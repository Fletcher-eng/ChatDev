# Topic 2 flashcards

#flashcards/biology/topic2

Each line is one card: question, then two colons, then the answer. Install the **Spaced Repetition** community plugin to review them, or just cover the answers.

What is the function of mitochondria?::Aerobic respiration, which releases energy.

What is the function of ribosomes?::Protein synthesis.

Name three structures found in plant cells but not animal cells.::Cell wall, chloroplasts and a large permanent vacuole.

What do bacteria have instead of a nucleus?::A single circular chromosome (circular DNA) and small rings of DNA called plasmids.

What does the cell membrane do?::Controls what enters and leaves the cell.

Give the magnification formula.::magnification = image size ÷ actual size

How many µm are in 1 mm?::1000 µm

Define tissue.::A group of cells with similar structures, working together to perform a shared function.

Define organ.::A structure made of a group of tissues, working together to perform specific functions.

What is the function of ciliated cells?::Moving mucus in the trachea and bronchi.

What is the function of palisade mesophyll cells?::Photosynthesis.
