# Topic 3 exam questions

For [[Biology 3 Movement into and out of cells]]. Write your answer under each question, then open the mark scheme and tick the points you made. These are original Specpoint questions in the Cambridge style, not official past paper questions.

## Written questions

### Question 1 · 3.1 · [2 marks]
Define the term diffusion.

**My answer:**


> [!check]- Mark scheme
> - [ ] Net movement of particles from a region of higher concentration to a region of lower concentration
> - [ ] Down a concentration gradient, as a result of random movement

### Question 2 · 3.1 · [2 marks]
State two changes that would increase the rate of diffusion of oxygen into a cell.

**My answer:**


> [!check]- Mark scheme
> - [ ] Larger surface area
> - [ ] Higher temperature
> - [ ] Steeper concentration gradient
> - [ ] Shorter diffusion distance
>
> *Any two, maximum 2 marks*

### Question 3 · 3.2 · Supplement · [3 marks]
A plant cell is placed in a concentrated salt solution. Describe and explain what happens to the cell.

**My answer:**


> [!check]- Mark scheme
> - [ ] Water leaves the cell by osmosis
> - [ ] From higher water potential inside the cell to lower water potential outside
> - [ ] Through the partially permeable membrane
> - [ ] Cell becomes flaccid
> - [ ] Membrane pulls away from the cell wall (plasmolysis)
>
> *Maximum 3 marks*

### Question 4 · 3.2 · [2 marks]
A potato cylinder has a mass of 2.50 g. After 30 minutes in a sucrose solution its mass is 2.20 g. Calculate the percentage change in mass.

**My answer:**


> [!check]- Mark scheme
> - [ ] 0.30 ÷ 2.50 × 100
> - [ ] 12% decrease (or a change of minus 12%)

### Question 5 · 3.3 · [2 marks]
Explain why root hair cells must use active transport to absorb mineral ions from the soil.

**My answer:**


> [!check]- Mark scheme
> - [ ] Concentration of ions in the soil is lower than in the cell, so ions move against the concentration gradient
> - [ ] Needs energy from respiration

### Question 6 · 3.3 · Supplement · [1 mark]
State the role of protein carriers in active transport.

**My answer:**


> [!check]- Mark scheme
> - [ ] They move molecules or ions across the membrane

## Multiple choice

### MC 1
Diffusion is the net movement of particles…

A. From higher to lower concentration, down a gradient
B. From lower to higher concentration, using energy
C. Only of water, across a membrane
D. Against a gradient, using carrier proteins

> [!success]- Answer
> **A. From higher to lower concentration, down a gradient**
> Net movement, higher to lower concentration, down a gradient, random movement.

### MC 2
Where does the energy for diffusion come from?

A. Respiration in mitochondria
B. Ribosomes
C. Light energy
D. Kinetic energy of moving particles

> [!success]- Answer
> **D. Kinetic energy of moving particles**
> Diffusion is passive. The energy is the kinetic energy of the particles.

### MC 3
Osmosis is the net movement of…

A. Any particles down a gradient
B. Ions against a gradient
C. Water through a partially permeable membrane
D. Glucose through a partially permeable membrane

> [!success]- Answer
> **C. Water through a partially permeable membrane**
> Osmosis is water only, through a partially permeable membrane.

### MC 4
A plant cell is put in a concentrated sugar solution. What happens?

A. Nothing, the cell wall stops it
B. It loses water and becomes flaccid
C. It gains water and bursts
D. It gains water and becomes turgid

> [!success]- Answer
> **B. It loses water and becomes flaccid**
> Water leaves by osmosis. The cell becomes flaccid, then plasmolysed.

### MC 5
A red blood cell is placed in pure water. What happens?

A. It gains water and bursts
B. It loses water and shrinks
C. It becomes plasmolysed
D. It stays the same

> [!success]- Answer
> **A. It gains water and bursts**
> Water enters by osmosis and there is no cell wall, so it bursts.

### MC 6
Which process moves substances AGAINST a concentration gradient?

A. Diffusion
B. Osmosis
C. Evaporation
D. Active transport

> [!success]- Answer
> **D. Active transport**
> Active transport uses energy from respiration.

### MC 7
Which is an example of active transport?

A. Water entering a root hair cell
B. Carbon dioxide entering a leaf
C. Root hair cells taking in mineral ions
D. Oxygen entering the blood in the lungs

> [!success]- Answer
> **C. Root hair cells taking in mineral ions**
> The soil has fewer ions than the cell, so energy is needed.

### MC 8
Which change INCREASES the rate of diffusion?

A. Lower concentration gradient
B. Higher temperature
C. Longer diffusion distance
D. Smaller surface area

> [!success]- Answer
> **B. Higher temperature**
> Higher temperature gives particles more kinetic energy.

### MC 9
Why does a plant wilt when short of water?

A. Its cells lose turgor pressure and become flaccid
B. Its cell walls dissolve
C. Its cells burst
D. It stops respiring

> [!success]- Answer
> **A. Its cells lose turgor pressure and become flaccid**
> Turgid cells support the plant. Flaccid cells cannot.
