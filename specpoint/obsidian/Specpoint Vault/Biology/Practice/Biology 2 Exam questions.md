### Question 5 · 2.2 · [2 marks]

**My answer:**


> [!check]- Mark scheme
> - [ ] 36 ÷ 0.09
> - [ ] ×400

### Question 6 · 2.2 · Supplement · [3 marks]

**My answer:**

## Multiple choice

### MC 1
Which structure is found in plant cells but NOT in animal cells?

A. Cell wall
B. Cell membrane
C. Ribosome
D. Nucleus

> [!success]- Answer
> **A. Cell wall**
> Cell wall, chloroplasts and a large permanent vacuole are the plant only structures here.

### MC 2
Where in the cell does aerobic respiration take place?

A. Ribosomes
B. Nucleus
C. Chloroplasts
D. Mitochondria

> [!success]- Answer
> **D. Mitochondria**
> Mitochondria release energy by aerobic respiration.

### MC 3
Which part controls what enters and leaves the cell?

A. Cytoplasm
B. Vacuole
C. Cell membrane
D. Cell wall

> [!success]- Answer
> **C. Cell membrane**
> The cell wall is fully permeable. Only the membrane controls entry.

### MC 4
Which of these is found in a bacterial cell?

A. Chloroplasts
B. Plasmids
C. A nucleus
D. Mitochondria

> [!success]- Answer
> **B. Plasmids**
> Bacteria have plasmids and circular DNA but no nucleus.

### MC 5
What is a plant cell wall made of?

A. Cellulose
B. Starch
C. Protein
D. Glycogen

> [!success]- Answer
> **A. Cellulose**
> Plant cell walls are made of cellulose.

### MC 6
Ciliated cells in the airways are specialised to…

A. Absorb oxygen
B. Carry oxygen
C. Make antibodies
D. Move mucus

> [!success]- Answer
> **D. Move mucus**
> Cilia beat to move mucus away from the lungs.

### MC 7
Which order of organisation is correct?

A. Cell, organ, tissue, organ system
B. Organ, tissue, cell, organ system
C. Cell, tissue, organ, organ system
D. Tissue, cell, organ, organ system

> [!success]- Answer
> **C. Cell, tissue, organ, organ system**
> Small to big: cell, tissue, organ, organ system, organism.

## Quick calculations

### Calculation 1
A drawing is 40 mm long. The real cell is 0.1 mm long. What is the magnification?

> [!success]- Answer
> **400 ×**. 40 ÷ 0.1 = ×400.

### Calculation 2
Convert 2.5 mm into µm.

> [!success]- Answer
> **2500 µm**. Multiply by 1000: 2500 µm.

### Calculation 3
An image is 15 mm long at ×300. What is the actual size in µm?

> [!success]- Answer
> **50 µm**. 15 ÷ 300 = 0.05 mm, then × 1000 = 50 µm.

### Calculation 4
A cell drawing is 60 mm wide. The real cell is 20 µm wide. What is the magnification?

> [!success]- Answer
> **3000 ×**. 20 µm = 0.02 mm. 60 ÷ 0.02 = ×3000.
