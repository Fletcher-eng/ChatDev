# Osmosis

> [!abstract] Definition
> The diffusion of water through a partially permeable membrane. Supplement definition: the net movement of water molecules from a region of higher water potential (a dilute solution) to a region of lower water potential (a concentrated solution), through a partially permeable membrane.
