# Cell wall

> [!abstract] Definition
> A tough outer layer that strengthens the cell and keeps its shape. It is fully permeable, so it does not control what enters the cell. In plants it is made of cellulose.

**Found in:** Plant cells and bacteria (not made of cellulose in bacteria)
