# Unit 2 flashcards

#flashcards/gp/unit2

Each line is one card: question, then two colons, then the answer. Install the **Spaced Repetition** community plugin to review them, or just cover the answers.

What is credibility?::Whether the author or source is believable, based on who made it and why.

What is reliability?::Whether the information can be trusted, based on its evidence and whether other sources agree.

What is a vested interest?::When the author gains something if people believe them, such as money or support.

What is bias?::Showing only one side of an issue, or favouring one view unfairly.

What does CRAAP stand for?::Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, Purpose.

What is a value judgement?::An opinion about whether something is good, bad, right or wrong. Look for words like should and must.

What is a fact?::A claim that can be checked and proved true or false.

What is a prediction?::A claim about what will or may happen in the future.

What is reasoning?::How evidence is linked to a conclusion.

Give a good reason a source is credible::The author has expertise, for example they are a scientist working in that field.
