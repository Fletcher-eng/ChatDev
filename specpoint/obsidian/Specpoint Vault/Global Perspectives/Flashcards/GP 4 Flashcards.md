# Unit 4 flashcards

#flashcards/gp/unit4

Each line is one card: question, then two colons, then the answer. Install the **Spaced Repetition** community plugin to review them, or just cover the answers.

What is primary research?::Information you collect yourself, such as surveys and interviews.

What is secondary research?::Information someone else has already collected, such as books, reports and websites.

What is quantitative data?::Data in numbers.

What is qualitative data?::Data in words, views and descriptions.

What makes a good research question?::It is focused and open, for example starting with "To what extent…".

What is a sample?::The group of people you collect data from. Bigger and more varied is more reliable.

Name the six GP skills::Research, analysis, evaluation, reflection, collaboration, communication.

What is reflection?::Thinking about what you learned, how your views changed and what you would do differently.

What does the command word Evaluate mean?::Judge strengths and weaknesses, then reach a conclusion.

What does the command word Justify mean?::Support your view with evidence and reasons.

What are the three parts of GP assessment?::The written exam, the Individual Report and the Team Project.
