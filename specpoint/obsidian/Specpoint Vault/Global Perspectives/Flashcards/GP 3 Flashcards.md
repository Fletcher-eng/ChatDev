# Unit 3 flashcards

#flashcards/gp/unit3

Each line is one card: question, then two colons, then the answer. Install the **Spaced Repetition** community plugin to review them, or just cover the answers.

What is an issue?::A problem or question that people disagree about or that needs action.

What is a perspective?::A point of view on an issue, shaped by things like location, job, culture and values, and supported by reasoning.

Name the four levels of perspective::Personal, local, national, global.

What is a stakeholder?::A person or group affected by an issue or involved in it.

What is a cause?::A reason why an issue happens.

What is a consequence?::A result or effect of an issue.

What is a course of action?::A possible way to tackle an issue.

What does PESTLE stand for?::Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental.

Five questions to judge a course of action::Will it work? Cost? Who gains and who loses? How long? Any side effects?
