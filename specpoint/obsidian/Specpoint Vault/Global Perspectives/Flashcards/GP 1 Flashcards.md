# Unit 1 flashcards

#flashcards/gp/unit1

Each line is one card: question, then two colons, then the answer. Install the **Spaced Repetition** community plugin to review them, or just cover the answers.

What does SEEEL stand for?::Statement, Evidence, Explain, Evaluate, Link.

What goes in the Statement?::One clear point that answers the question.

What is the difference between Explain and Evaluate?::Explain says how the evidence proves your point. Evaluate judges how strong or reliable the evidence is, or brings in another perspective.

Sentence starter for Evidence::"According to …" or "For example, …"

Sentence starter for Explain::"This shows that … because …"

Sentence starter for Evaluate::"However, this evidence may be limited because …"

Sentence starter for Link::"Therefore, …" or "Overall, this suggests that …"

What is a counter argument?::A reason against your view. Answering it makes your own argument stronger.

What is a conclusion?::The judgement you reach at the end, which follows from your reasons.

What is PEEL?::Point, Evidence, Explain, Link. SEEEL adds Evaluate.
