# Unit 1 exam questions

For [[GP 1 Writing arguments]]. Write your answer under each question, then open the mark scheme and tick the points you made. These are original Specpoint questions in the Cambridge style, not official past paper questions.

## Written questions

### Question 1 · 1.1 · [6 marks]
Write one SEEEL paragraph to answer this question: Should mobile phones be banned in schools?

### Question 2 · 1.1 · [2 marks]
Explain the difference between explaining evidence and evaluating evidence.

**My answer:**


> [!check]- Mark scheme
> - [ ] Explaining shows how the evidence supports the point
> - [ ] Evaluating judges how strong or reliable the evidence is, or considers its limits

### Question 3 · 1.2 · [2 marks]
What is a counter argument, and why does including one make an argument stronger?

**My answer:**


> [!check]- Mark scheme
> - [ ] A reason or argument against your view
> - [ ] Answering it shows you have considered other views, so your conclusion is better supported

## Multiple choice

### MC 1
What does the L in SEEEL stand for?

A. Link
B. List
C. Logic
D. Learn

> [!success]- Answer
> **A. Link**
> Link brings the paragraph back to the question.

### MC 2
Which part of SEEEL comes straight after the Statement?

A. Explain
B. Evaluate
C. Link
D. Evidence

> [!success]- Answer
> **D. Evidence**
> Statement, Evidence, Explain, Evaluate, Link.

### MC 3
"This shows that … because …" starts which part?

A. Evaluate
B. Statement
C. Explain
D. Evidence

> [!success]- Answer
> **C. Explain**
> Explain says how the evidence proves your point.

### MC 4
"However, this evidence may be limited because …" starts which part?

A. Evidence
B. Evaluate
C. Explain
D. Link

> [!success]- Answer
> **B. Evaluate**
> Evaluate judges how strong the evidence is.

### MC 5
PEEL is missing which part of SEEEL?

A. Evaluate
B. Evidence
C. Explain
D. Link

> [!success]- Answer
> **A. Evaluate**
> PEEL is Point, Evidence, Explain, Link.

### MC 6
What makes an argument stronger?

A. Ignoring other views
B. Repeating your point
C. Using longer words
D. Answering a counter argument

> [!success]- Answer
> **D. Answering a counter argument**
> Showing why the other side is weaker makes your conclusion more convincing.

### MC 7
Where does your final judgement go?

A. The evidence
B. The title
C. The conclusion
D. The statement

> [!success]- Answer
> **C. The conclusion**
> The conclusion is the judgement you reach from your reasons.

### MC 8
Which is the best Statement for "Should homework be banned?"

A. Therefore homework is complicated.
B. One reason homework should be banned is that it causes stress.
C. Homework is a thing schools give.
D. Research found students do 5 hours a week.

> [!success]- Answer
> **B. One reason homework should be banned is that it causes stress.**
> A Statement makes one clear point that answers the question.
