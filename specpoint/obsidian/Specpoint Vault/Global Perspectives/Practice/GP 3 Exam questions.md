# Unit 3 exam questions

## Written questions

### Question 1 · 3.1 · [4 marks]
Explain why two different stakeholders might have different perspectives on building a new airport near a town.

**My answer:**


> [!check]- Mark scheme
> - [ ] Identifies one stakeholder and their view, for example a local business supports it
> - [ ] Gives a reason, for example more customers, jobs or trade
> - [ ] Identifies a second stakeholder with a different view, for example nearby residents oppose it
> - [ ] Gives a reason, for example noise or air pollution

### Question 2 · 3.2 · [3 marks]
Suggest one course of action to reduce plastic waste and explain how it would help.

**My answer:**


> [!check]- Mark scheme
> - [ ] A realistic course of action, for example a charge on plastic bags
> - [ ] Explains how it reduces the problem, for example people reuse bags
> - [ ] Considers a limitation or who it affects

### Question 3 · 3.2 · [2 marks]
Explain one economic consequence of water shortages for a local community.

**My answer:**


> [!check]- Mark scheme
> - [ ] Identifies an economic consequence, for example crops fail or water prices rise
> - [ ] Explains the effect on people, for example farmers lose income

## Multiple choice

### MC 1
Which is a global perspective?

A. Rising sea levels threaten coastal cities worldwide.
B. My street floods every winter.
C. Our town built a new sea wall.
D. The government raised flood defence spending.

> [!success]- Answer
> **A. Rising sea levels threaten coastal cities worldwide.**
> Global means the whole world.

### MC 2
A person or group affected by an issue is a…

A. Perspective
B. Consequence
C. Source
D. Stakeholder

> [!success]- Answer
> **D. Stakeholder**
> Stakeholders can hold different perspectives.

### MC 3
Which two PESTLE factors start with E?

A. Environmental and Educational
B. Energy and Economic
C. Economic and Environmental
D. Ethical and Economic

> [!success]- Answer
> **C. Economic and Environmental**
> Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental.

### MC 4
Which order goes from smallest to largest scale?

A. National, local, personal, global
B. Personal, local, national, global
C. Local, personal, global, national
D. Global, national, local, personal

> [!success]- Answer
> **B. Personal, local, national, global**
> You, your town, your country, the world.

### MC 5
A possible way to tackle an issue is a…

A. Course of action
B. Cause
C. Consequence
D. Claim

> [!success]- Answer
> **A. Course of action**
> Always explain how it would solve the issue.

### MC 6
"More people drive cars" is best described as a…

A. Consequence of air pollution
B. Course of action
C. Perspective
D. Cause of air pollution

> [!success]- Answer
> **D. Cause of air pollution**
> It is a reason why the issue happens.

### MC 7
"Fewer tourists visit polluted beaches" is which type of consequence?

A. Political
B. Technological
C. Economic
D. Legal

> [!success]- Answer
> **C. Economic**
> Lost tourism means lost money and jobs.
