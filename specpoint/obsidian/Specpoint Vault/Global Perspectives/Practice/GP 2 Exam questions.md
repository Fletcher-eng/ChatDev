## Multiple choice

### MC 1
What does the C in CRAAP stand for?

A. Currency
B. Credibility
C. Claim
D. Content

> [!success]- Answer
> **A. Currency**
> Currency means how up to date the source is.

### MC 2
A source written by a company about its own product has a…

A. Prediction
B. Value judgement
C. Primary source
D. Vested interest

> [!success]- Answer
> **D. Vested interest**
> The company gains if you believe it.

### MC 3
Which of these is a fact?

A. Everyone should recycle.
B. Electric cars will replace all petrol cars.
C. Water boils at 100°C at sea level.
D. Cities are nicer than villages.

> [!success]- Answer
> **C. Water boils at 100°C at sea level.**
> A fact can be checked and proved.

### MC 4
Which word most often signals a value judgement?

A. Recorded
B. Should
C. Is
D. Measured

> [!success]- Answer
> **B. Should**
> Should, must and fair show a view about what is right.

### MC 5
Which makes a source more credible?

A. The author is an expert in the field
B. It has lots of adverts
C. It is very long
D. It only gives one side

> [!success]- Answer
> **A. The author is an expert in the field**
> Expertise raises credibility.

### MC 6
"By 2050 most cars will be electric" is a…

A. Fact
B. Value judgement
C. Statistic
D. Prediction

> [!success]- Answer
> **D. Prediction**
> It is a claim about the future.

### MC 7
Showing only one side of an issue is called…

A. Reasoning
B. Currency
C. Bias
D. Evidence

> [!success]- Answer
> **C. Bias**
> A biased source favours one view unfairly.

### MC 8
How evidence is linked to a conclusion is called…

A. A claim
B. Reasoning
C. Currency
D. Bias

> [!success]- Answer
> **B. Reasoning**
> Good reasoning is logical and does not jump to conclusions.
