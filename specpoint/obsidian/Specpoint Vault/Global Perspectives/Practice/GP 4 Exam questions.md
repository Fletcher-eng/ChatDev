# Unit 4 exam questions

For [[GP 4 Research and assessment]]. Write your answer under each question, then open the mark scheme and tick the points you made. These are original Specpoint questions in the Cambridge style, not official past paper questions.

## Written questions

### Question 1 · 4.1 · [2 marks]
Explain the difference between primary and secondary research.

**My answer:**


> [!check]- Mark scheme
> - [ ] Primary research is collected by you, for example a survey
> - [ ] Secondary research has already been collected by someone else, for example a report

### Question 2 · 4.1 · [2 marks]
Give one advantage and one disadvantage of using a questionnaire for research.

**My answer:**


> [!check]- Mark scheme
> - [ ] Advantage: collects lots of answers quickly, or gives data that is easy to compare
> - [ ] Disadvantage: people may not answer honestly, answers can be short, or questions may be leading

### Question 3 · 4.2 · [2 marks]
What does the command word evaluate ask you to do?

**My answer:**


> [!check]- Mark scheme
> - [ ] Judge strengths and weaknesses, or how good something is
> - [ ] Reach a conclusion or overall judgement

## Multiple choice

### MC 1
A survey you write and hand out yourself is…

A. Primary research
B. Secondary research
C. A prediction
D. Bias

> [!success]- Answer
> **A. Primary research**
> Primary means you collected it.

### MC 2
"68% of students walk to school" is…

A. Qualitative data
B. A value judgement
C. A course of action
D. Quantitative data

> [!success]- Answer
> **D. Quantitative data**
> It is in numbers.

### MC 3
Which is NOT one of the six GP skills?

A. Collaboration
B. Analysis
C. Memorisation
D. Reflection

> [!success]- Answer
> **C. Memorisation**
> The six are research, analysis, evaluation, reflection, collaboration and communication.

### MC 4
Which command word means support your view with evidence?

A. Compare
B. Justify
C. Identify
D. Describe

> [!success]- Answer
> **B. Justify**
> Justify means back it up.

### MC 5
Thinking about how your views changed is called…

A. Reflection
B. Research
C. Communication
D. Analysis

> [!success]- Answer
> **A. Reflection**
> Reflection is a key part of the Team Project.

### MC 6
Which is the best research question?

A. Is plastic bad?
B. Plastic.
C. What year was plastic invented?
D. To what extent should my school ban single use plastic?

> [!success]- Answer
> **D. To what extent should my school ban single use plastic?**
> Good research questions are focused and open.

### MC 7
Why avoid leading questions in a survey?

A. They are too short
B. They give numbers
C. They push people towards an answer
D. They take too long

> [!success]- Answer
> **C. They push people towards an answer**
> Leading questions make your results less reliable.
