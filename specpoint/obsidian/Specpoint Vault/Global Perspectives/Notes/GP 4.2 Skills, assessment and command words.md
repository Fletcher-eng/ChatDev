# 4.2 Skills, assessment and command words

Part of [[GP 4 Research and assessment]] in [[Global Perspectives]]. Written by the Specpoint team and based on the Cambridge 0457 skills.

### The six GP skills

Everything in GP tests six skills: [[Research]], [[Analysis]], [[Evaluation]], [[Reflection]], [[Collaboration]] and [[Communication]]. [[Reflection]] means thinking about what you learned, how your views changed, and what you would do differently next time.

### How you are assessed

| Part | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| Written exam | Answer questions about sources on global issues: analyse claims, judge evidence and build arguments. |
| Individual Report | Research a global issue on your own and write a report that explores different perspectives and reaches a conclusion. |
| Team Project | Work in a team on a project about a local issue, then reflect on your own work and the team's work. |

Exam timings, word limits and deadlines can change between years, so check the details for your year with your teacher.

### Command words

| Word | What it asks you to do |
| --- | --- |
| Identify | Name or pick out something |
| Explain | Give reasons, saying how or why, with evidence |
| Compare | Point out similarities and differences |
| Evaluate | Judge strengths and weaknesses, then reach a conclusion |
| Justify | Support your view with evidence and reasons |
| Suggest | Put forward a sensible idea and back it up |

> [!question] Quick check
> Which command word asks you to judge strengths and weaknesses and reach a conclusion?
>
> A. Identify
> B. Describe
> C. State
> D. Evaluate
>
> > [!success]- Answer
> > **Evaluate**. Evaluate means judge, then decide.
