# 3.1 Perspectives

Part of [[GP 3 Issues and perspectives]] in [[Global Perspectives]]. Written by the Specpoint team and based on the Cambridge 0457 skills.

An [[Issue]] is a problem or question that people disagree about or that needs action. A [[Perspective]] is a point of view on an issue, shaped by things like where someone lives, their job, their culture and their values, and supported by reasoning.

### Levels of perspective

GP asks you to look at an issue at different scales. Here is the issue of water shortages:

| Level | Example |
| --- | --- |
| [[Personal perspective]] | "My family has to take shorter showers." |
| [[Local perspective]] | "Our town has banned using hosepipes in summer." |
| [[National perspective]] | "The government is building plants to turn sea water into drinking water." |
| [[Global perspective]] | "Climate change is making droughts more common in many regions." |

A [[Stakeholder]] is any person or group affected by an issue or involved in it. Different stakeholders often hold different perspectives.

> [!tip] Examiner tip
> When you compare perspectives, say **why** each group holds its view. "Farmers support the dam because it gives them water for crops" is far stronger than "farmers like the dam".

> [!question] Quick check
> "Climate change is making droughts more common in many regions" is a view at which level?
>
> A. Personal
> B. Local
> C. National
> D. Global
>
> > [!success]- Answer
> > **Global**. It describes the whole world, so it is a global perspective.
