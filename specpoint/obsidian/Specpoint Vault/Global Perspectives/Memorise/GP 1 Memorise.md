## SEEEL

*How to build a paragraph*

**S E E E L**

| Letter | Stands for | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **S** | Statement | Make one clear point |
| **E** | Evidence | Back it up with a source |
| **E** | Explain | Say how it proves your point |
| **E** | Evaluate | Judge how strong it is |
| **L** | Link | Back to the question |

> [!question]- Test yourself: what does each letter stand for?
> **S**: Statement
> **E**: Evidence
> **E**: Explain
> **E**: Evaluate
> **L**: Link

What does SEEEL stand for?::Statement, Evidence, Explain, Evaluate, Link

SEEEL: the S::Statement (Make one clear point)

SEEEL: the first E::Evidence (Back it up with a source)

SEEEL: the second E::Explain (Say how it proves your point)

SEEEL: the third E::Evaluate (Judge how strong it is)

SEEEL: the L::Link (Back to the question)

### Example paragraph in SEEEL order

**Question:** Should governments ban single use plastic bags?

1. **Statement:** One reason governments should ban single use plastic bags is that they harm wildlife in the oceans.
2. **Evidence:** The United Nations Environment Programme reports that plastic pollution is a serious threat to marine animals.
3. **Explain:** This shows that plastic bags damage ecosystems that many communities rely on for food and jobs.
4. **Evaluate:** However, the report covers all plastic waste, not only bags, so a ban on bags alone may have a smaller effect.
5. **Link:** Therefore, a ban is a useful first step, but it works best alongside wider action on plastic.
