## The six skills

*What every part of GP tests*

**R A E R C C**

| Letter | Stands for | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **R** | Research | Finding information |
| **A** | Analysis | Breaking ideas down |
| **E** | Evaluation | Judging how strong something is |
| **R** | Reflection | Thinking about what you learned |
| **C** | Collaboration | Working in a team |
| **C** | Communication | Getting ideas across clearly |
