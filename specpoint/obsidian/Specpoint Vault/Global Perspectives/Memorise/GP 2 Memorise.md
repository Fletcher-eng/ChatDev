## CRAAP

*Checking a source*

**C R A A P**

| Letter | Stands for | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **C** | Currency | How up to date it is |
| **R** | Relevance | Does it answer your question |
| **A** | Authority | Who wrote it |
| **A** | Accuracy | Is it backed by evidence |
| **P** | Purpose | Why it was written |

> [!question]- Test yourself: what does each letter stand for?
> **C**: Currency
> **R**: Relevance
> **A**: Authority
> **A**: Accuracy
> **P**: Purpose

What does CRAAP stand for?::Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, Purpose

CRAAP: the C::Currency (How up to date it is)

CRAAP: the R::Relevance (Does it answer your question)

CRAAP: the first A::Authority (Who wrote it)

CRAAP: the second A::Accuracy (Is it backed by evidence)

CRAAP: the P::Purpose (Why it was written)
