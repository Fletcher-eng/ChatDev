## PESTLE

*Ways to find causes and consequences*

**P E S T L E**

| Letter | Stands for | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **P** | Political | Governments and laws being made |
| **E** | Economic | Money, jobs and trade |
| **S** | Social | People, health and communities |
| **T** | Technological | New inventions and tools |
| **L** | Legal | Laws that already exist |
| **E** | Environmental | Nature and the planet |

> [!question]- Test yourself: what does each letter stand for?
> **P**: Political
> **E**: Economic
> **S**: Social
> **T**: Technological
> **L**: Legal
> **E**: Environmental

What does PESTLE stand for?::Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental

PESTLE: the P::Political (Governments and laws being made)

PESTLE: the first E::Economic (Money, jobs and trade)

PESTLE: the S::Social (People, health and communities)

PESTLE: the T::Technological (New inventions and tools)

PESTLE: the L::Legal (Laws that already exist)

PESTLE: the second E::Environmental (Nature and the planet)
