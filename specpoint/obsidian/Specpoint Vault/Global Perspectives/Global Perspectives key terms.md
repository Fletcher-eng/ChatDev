# Global Perspectives key terms

Every key term in the Specpoint Global Perspectives notes. Learn these words exactly. Examiners reward the right terms.

### A
- [[Analysis]]: One of the six GP skills: breaking an issue or argument down to see its parts and how they link.
- [[Argument]]: A set of reasons that lead to a conclusion.

### B
- [[Bias]]: Showing only one side of an issue, or favouring one view unfairly.

### C
- [[Cause]]: A reason why an issue happens.
- [[Claim]]: A statement that something is true or should happen.
- [[Collaboration]]: One of the six GP skills: working well in a team towards a shared goal.
- [[Communication]]: One of the six GP skills: presenting ideas clearly and in an organised way.
- [[Conclusion]]: The judgement you reach at the end.
- [[Consequence]]: A result or effect of an issue.
- [[Counter argument]]: A reason against your view.
- [[Course of action]]: A possible way to tackle an issue.
- [[CRAAP test]]: A checklist for judging sources: Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, Purpose.
- [[Credibility]]: Whether the author or source is believable, based on who made it and why.

### E
- [[Evaluation]]: One of the six GP skills: judging the strengths, weaknesses and reliability of evidence and arguments.
- [[Evidence]]: Information used to support a claim, such as data, examples or expert views.
- [[Expertise]]: Knowledge or qualifications in a subject.

### F
- [[Fact]]: A claim that can be checked and proved true or false.

### G
- [[Global perspective]]: How an issue affects or is seen by the whole world.

### I
- [[Issue]]: A problem or question that people disagree about or that needs action.

### L
- [[Local perspective]]: How an issue affects your town or community.

### N
- [[National perspective]]: How an issue affects or is seen by a whole country.

### O
- [[Opinion]]: A personal belief or view.

### P
- [[PEEL]]: Point, Evidence, Explain, Link.
- [[Personal perspective]]: How an issue affects you and your family.
- [[Perspective]]: A point of view on an issue, shaped by things like location, job, culture and values, and supported by reasoning.
- [[PESTLE]]: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental.
- [[Prediction]]: A claim about what will or may happen in the future.
- [[Primary research]]: Information you collect yourself, such as surveys, interviews and observations.

### Q
- [[Qualitative data]]: Data in words, views and descriptions.
- [[Quantitative data]]: Data in numbers.

### R
- [[Reasoning]]: How evidence is linked to a conclusion.
- [[Reflection]]: One of the six GP skills: thinking about what you learned, how your views changed and what you would do differently.
- [[Reliability]]: Whether information can be trusted, based on the evidence it uses and whether other sources agree.
- [[Research]]: One of the six GP skills: finding and selecting information to answer a question.
- [[Research question]]: The focused, open question that guides your research, for example one starting with To what extent.

### S
- [[Sample]]: The group of people you collect data from.
- [[Secondary research]]: Information someone else has already collected, such as books, reports and websites.
- [[SEEEL]]: A way to structure a paragraph: Statement, Evidence, Explain, Evaluate, Link.
- [[Stakeholder]]: A person or group affected by an issue or involved in it.

### V
- [[Value judgement]]: An opinion about whether something is good, bad, right or wrong.
- [[Vested interest]]: When the author gains something if people believe them, such as money or support.
